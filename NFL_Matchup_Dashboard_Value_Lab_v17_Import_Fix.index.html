How the averages and rankings are calculated

Each player-game is assigned to the player's offensive team, the opponent's defensive opponent_team, the player's position, season and week. Player production is summed into team/position/game totals. A team's positional season average is the total divided by all games played by the team, including games with zero contributions at that position. Defensive allowance uses all games played by that defending team and sums the opponent's players in the relevant position. Last four uses each team's last four played games before the selected week, excluding byes. Rankings compare 32 teams within the same position and statistical category; a team's own offensive rank and its opponent's allowed rank each contribute 50% to the opportunity score. Equal values share their competition rank. Total offensive yards adds passing yards from all QB/RB/WR/TE passers to rushing yards from all those positions; receiving yards are not added again. Total defensive yards allowed is the same sum credited to the opposing defence. The weekly and individual game narratives are rule-based and calculated locally from these values, not a generative AI service. QB passing, QB rushing, RB rushing, RB receiving, WR receiving and TE receiving are kept separate; QB passing yards need not match total receiving yards.

Original CSV: nflverse weekly player stats 2026. Source code and definitions: nflverse-data and player-stat data dictionary. Schedule: GitHub schedule mirror. The 2026 GitHub schedule provides actual final team scores for total points scored and allowed. Kicking points require a separate imported team-week CSV with made field goals and extra points. A team’s offensive TDs equal rushing TDs plus receiving TDs in the player dataset; this excludes special-teams and defensive scores. Passing TDs are not added because that would double-count receiving TDs. Kicking points allowed remain unavailable without opponent kicking records.
